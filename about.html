About MindCare

Built to help students break the ice with their school psychologist.

MindCare is a mobile application for students ages 13–18 in participating schools. Its goal is simple: make students feel more comfortable starting a conversation with the school psychologist and following supportive activities.

MindCare objective: support student mental health by making the first message to the school psychologist feel easier, calmer, and less intimidating.

🎓

For school communities

MindCare is designed around school-managed access and psychologist-led student support workflows.

🧭

For students

Students get a quiet space for mood check-ins, supportive activities, and appropriate communication with a school psychologist.

🔍

For app information

Public pages explain privacy, account deletion, intended audience, and safety expectations in plain language.

What MindCare is

A bridge between students and school psychologists.

MindCare helps students start conversations, share mood reflections, and complete activities assigned by their school psychologist. The app supports the relationship between student and psychologist instead of replacing it.

What schools control

School access and privacy must stay clear.

Participating schools manage student access, psychologist workflows, consent requirements, urgent-risk procedures, and account deletion or retention rules for the mobile app.
