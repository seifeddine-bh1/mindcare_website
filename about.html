About MindCare

Built to make school support easier to find, safer to explain, and calmer to use.

MindCare is a student well-being companion for participating schools. It gives students ages 13–18 a guided place to reflect, message school psychologists, and follow support activities assigned by care teams.

Brand promise: a warm, non-clinical experience that communicates trust before a student, family, school, or app reviewer takes the next step.

🎓

For school communities

MindCare is designed around school-managed access, school support workflows, and clear boundaries between well-being support and medical care.

🧭

For students

Students get a quiet space for mood check-ins, supportive activities, and appropriate communication with a school psychologist.

🔍

For review readiness

Public pages now explain privacy, account deletion, intended audience, limitations, and safety expectations in plain language.

What MindCare is

A support workflow, not a replacement for professionals.

MindCare helps organize communication, reflection, and follow-up between students and authorized school support staff. It can assist care conversations, but it does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or provide emergency response.

What schools control

Access, consent, escalation, and retention must match school policy.

Participating schools are responsible for configuring student access, obtaining required guardian consent, defining urgent-risk procedures, and approving retention or deletion processes before production rollout.
