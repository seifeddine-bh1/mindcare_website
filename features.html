Features

Student well-being tools with a calm, school-ready experience.

MindCare focuses on safe communication, supportive self-reflection, and psychologist-guided activities rather than diagnosis or emergency response.

💬

Secure psychologist messaging

Students can message their school psychologist through identified or anonymous modes, based on the school workflow.

📈

Mood tracking

Daily mood selections, tags, and optional notes help students reflect and help support teams understand trends.

✅

Assigned tasks

Psychologists can assign coping exercises, follow-up activities, and check-ins while monitoring completion.

🌿

Stress-relief activities

Guided activities encourage breathing, grounding, journaling, and other low-pressure well-being practices.

🛡️

Privacy controls

School-managed access, encrypted storage, and role-aware visibility are presented transparently for families.

🏫

School-centered setup

Students join through school codes, and participating schools manage access, support workflows, and consent processes.

Not a crisis service

MindCare does not provide emergency intervention, medical diagnosis, therapy, or treatment. Students in immediate danger should contact local emergency services, a trusted adult, or their school’s urgent safety process.
