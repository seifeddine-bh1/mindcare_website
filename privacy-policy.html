Privacy Policy

MindCare Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

MindCare is intended for students ages 13–18 in participating schools. It is not an emergency service and does not provide diagnosis, therapy, medical treatment, crisis monitoring, or 24/7 response.

1. Overview

MindCare helps students communicate with school psychologists, track mood, complete assigned tasks, and use supportive well-being activities. This policy explains how MindCare collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Birthdate or age range confirmation
  • School code and school association
  • Optional profile picture

Communication data

  • Messages exchanged between students and school psychologists
  • Anonymous or identified conversation setting selected in the app

Mood, task, and activity data

  • Daily mood selections, predefined tags, and optional notes
  • Tasks assigned by psychologists and completion status
  • Limited activity logs needed for reliability, debugging, and safety

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the MindCare app
  • Enable student-to-psychologist communication
  • Support mood tracking, tasks, and well-being activities
  • Maintain safety, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable obligations
  • Improve app performance and user experience

4. Sharing and visibility

MindCare does not sell personal data, use student data for behavioral advertising, or allow student-to-student public posting.

Data may be visible to authorized school psychologists and school administrators only as needed for the service. MindCare may also share limited information with technical service providers or legal authorities when required by law or needed to protect user safety.

Anonymous mode hides the student’s identity from the psychologist interface, but the system may still associate activity with an account internally for security, audit, and functionality purposes.

5. Retention and account deletion

MindCare keeps information only as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, support school safety processes, and maintain system integrity.

Users can delete their account directly inside the app. Deleted accounts are removed unless limited retention is legally or operationally required.

6. Security measures

MindCare uses safeguards such as encrypted storage, secure authentication, role-based access control, and restricted data visibility. Messages are stored in encrypted form using a cryptographic approach based on public/private keys. Private keys are protected by a separate encryption password when applicable, and MindCare does not store account passwords in plain text.

7. Children, schools, and consent

MindCare is used by minors ages 13–18 within educational institutions. Access is managed by participating schools. Schools are responsible for obtaining any required parent or guardian consent, configuring appropriate student support workflows, and defining crisis escalation procedures outside the app.

8. International and legal review note

This policy is written to explain expected product behavior in plain language. Schools and operators should have the policy reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions where the app is offered, including student privacy, education records, child privacy, and health-related rules that may apply.

9. Support and account deletion

For privacy questions or support, please use the MindCare app or the support process provided by the participating school. Account deletion is available directly inside the app.
